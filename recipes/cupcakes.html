Cupcake Recipe

Recipes

Description

This is my fav chocolate cupcake recipe! They are vegan cupcakes (I'm not vegan, so these being my fav should say a lot) and take less than an hour to make from start to finish. Personally, I do not like frosting, so no frosting will be included in this recipe, but if you disagree, feel free to add your fav homemade or store-bought frosting to this dish!

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cup all purpose flour
  • 1/2 cocoa powder
  • 1/2 cup white sugar
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 cup soy milk
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/3 cup canola oil
  • 3 tbsp applesauce
  • 1 tbsp distilled white vinegar

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350F
  2. Mix all ingredients into one big bowl
  3. Seperate batter into a lined cupcake baking tin
  4. Bake for 20 minutes
  5. Let cool, and enjoy!
