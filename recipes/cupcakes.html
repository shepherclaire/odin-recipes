Cupcake Recipe

Recipes

Description

This is my fav chocolate cupcake recipe! They are vegan cupcakes (I'm not vegan, so these being my fav should say a lot) and take less than an hour to make from start to finish. Personally, I do not like frosting, so no frosting will be included in this recipe, but if you disagree, feel free to add your fav homemade or store-bought frosting to this dish!
