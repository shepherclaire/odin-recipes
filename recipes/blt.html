BLT Recipe

Recipes

Description

This is my fav sandwhich recipe! Simple, American classic BLT. The recipe should be easy to follow as its ingredients are in the title - bacon, lettuce, and tomato (and bread and sauce) are the only ingredients you need to make a delicious meal!

Ingredients

  • 4 strips of bacon
  • 2 tomato slices (1/4-1/2 inch thick)
  • Lettuce to taste
  • 2 slices of bread
  • Mayo

Steps

  1. Pan fry bacon to desired crispyness and pat dry with a paper towel once cooked
  2. Toast bread (optional)
  3. Take both slices of bread and spread mayo on one side of each piece
  4. On one slice of bread mayo side up, layer the bacon, lettuce and tomato
  5. Place the second slice of bread onto of the stack
  6. Enjoy!
