BLT Recipe

Recipes

Description

This is my fav sandwhich recipe! Simple, American classic BLT. The recipe should be easy to follow as its ingredients are in the title - bacon, lettuce, and tomato (and bread and sauce) are the only ingredients you need to make a delicious meal!

Ingredients

  • 4 strips of bacon
  • 2 tomato slices (1/4-1/2 inch thick)
  • Lettuce to taste
  • 2 slices of bread
  • Mayo

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350F
  2. Mix all ingredients into one big bowl
  3. Seperate batter into a lined cupcake baking tin
  4. Bake for 20 minutes
  5. Let cool, and enjoy!
