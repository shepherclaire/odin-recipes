Ice Water Recipe

Recipes

Description

This is my fav drink recipe! Want something refreshing, plain, hydrating, and vegan? Look no further than this recipe as it is sure to satisfy your wildest cravings!

Ingredients

  • Ice (I enjoy 5-6 large cubes)
  • Water

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350F
  2. Mix all ingredients into one big bowl
  3. Seperate batter into a lined cupcake baking tin
  4. Bake for 20 minutes
  5. Let cool, and enjoy!
